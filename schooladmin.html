School Admin

You can see the dashboards and reports of every department of the school. Everything here is view only: you cannot add, change or delete records. Choose a department:

Registrar

Enrollment dashboard, registration and enrolled masterlists.

Finance

Collections, balances, payment transactions and the enrolled masterlist.

Payroll

Payroll dashboard (cash advances, loans, attendance) and reports.

HR

Employee dashboard, the list of employees and leave requests.

Custodian

Uniform, ID and item records, student files and monitoring.

Guidance

Health and guidance dashboard, records and student files.

Your own Leave request button (side panel) works as for every employee.
