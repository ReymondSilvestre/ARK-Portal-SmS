Registration masterlist

Every student who is registered, enrolled or not. Enrolled students are also on the Enrolled masterlist.

Remark / remove student

Open this from the Remark or Delete button of a student in the enrolled list. A remark (Transferred Out, Drop Out or Cancelled Enrolled) is saved on the student's enrollment record for that school year. When the box below is ticked, the student's login account is deleted as well, even if it was already created. The registration and enrollment records stay, so the history is kept.
