Create an employee account

The employee is registered first (by HR, or by you under Employee registration). You then choose that employee below and pick the account type. The employee signs in with the school code + Employee No.; the first password is the birth year + first letter of the last name (example 1985D) and must be changed at first sign-in.

First HR account. Nobody can add employees until there is an HR account, so type the HR person here. They are added to the HR records automatically.

If you enter a real email, the person can also sign in with it and Forgot your password? sends the link there. The employee must be Active in the HR records.

Student accounts are grouped by school year (the year the account was made) and then by section. Each section has buttons to set the password of the whole section at once.
