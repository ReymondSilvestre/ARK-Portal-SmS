SylverVibe Code - Exam Portal

Sign in

Sign in with your Exam ID No. (for example 2026-0001). No school code needed.

Teachers, Registrar, Finance, Custodian and Guidance: school code + Employee No. IT Admins: your email (leave the school code blank).

No account yet? Your school's IT Administrator creates teacher accounts, and your teacher creates student accounts.

Reset your password

A reset link is sent to the email address on your account (the one you gave when your account was created). Students: enter your Exam ID No. or email. Teachers and other staff: ask your school\u2019s IT Admin to email you a reset link.

Choose a new password

At least 10 characters with a letter and a number (students: at least 8).
